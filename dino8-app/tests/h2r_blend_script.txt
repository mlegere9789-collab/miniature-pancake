# BlendSrf records construction history (tests/smoke.sh section H2R_, part 4).
# With History On the blend surface is recorded as {surface A, surface B} with each
# boundary edge named intrinsically (isoparametric direction, min/max, face index) and
# the continuity; the two surfaces are NOT consumed. Moving either surface rebuilds the
# blend automatically, EditHistory changes the continuity, and a source that is no
# longer a surface makes the rebuild refuse and keep the old blend. Every claim is a
# measured Bounding box, compared with the SAME blend built fresh (History Off) between
# the surfaces in their moved positions.
# @TMP@ is the test temp directory.
SetActiveViewport Top

# ============================================================ B1: two coplanar squares -> BlendSrf
History On

Plane 20,0,0 30,10,0
