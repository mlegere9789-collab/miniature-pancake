# Tolerance-contract surface/curve tools (tests/smoke.sh section SOT_): RemoveMultiKnot, FitSrf, ShrinkTrimmedSrf.
# Every figure is MEASURED by the app (Length, ClosestPt, List, Area, BoundingBox) and compared in smoke.sh with a
# closed form or an independent bound. Cases in order (smoke.sh cuts the output at each "Command: RemoveMultiKnot"
# / "Command: FitSrf" / "Command: ShrinkTrimmedSrf" line):
#  RMK1 a smooth cubic with a degree-multiplicity knot inserted by InsertKink: removal is exact (Length unchanged)
#  RMK2 a right-angle polyline elevated to degree 3 (a genuine C0 corner, knot multiplicity 3): Tolerance 0.001 refused,
#       curve unchanged (Length 9); Tolerance 100 removes it, ClosestPt of the old corner (5,0,0) to the new curve is
#       positive (the corner really was smoothed) and <= the reported guaranteed bound
#  FIT1 a lofted bicubic (5 x 4 net, already the smallest net that reproduces it) with two knots inserted (7 x 6): FitSrf at
#       Tolerance 0.001 takes it back to 5 x 4 with measured deviation 0 and Area / BoundingBox unchanged; the minimal net is kept
#       at 0.02 and at 1e-6; an impossible tolerance with MaxPoints=4 is refused and reports the best deviation reached
#  SHR1 box minus cylinder (a sealed B-rep whose faces still carry their full box planes): ShrinkTrimmedSrf cuts the surfaces
#       down to their trims with Volume (1000 - 40 pi), Area (600 + 32 pi) and Check unchanged; a circle's planar surface has no
#       surface larger than its trims, so it is reported as such (NoOp) with Area (9 pi) and BoundingBox unchanged
