#title: SubD basics
#summary: Make a subdivision-surface box, crease an edge, extrude a face, and convert the result to exact NURBS.
#level: Intermediate
# Source: extends docs/tutorial_scripts/08_subd_basics.txt.
#narrate: Subdivision surfaces are for organic, smooth shapes. You edit a coarse cage and the program smooths it. We start with the simplest cage there is, a box.
SubDBox 0,0,0 10,10,0 10
@expect_objects 1
#narrate: SubDBox makes a ten unit cube as a SubD. Note how soft it looks: the corners are rounded by the smoothing.
SetActiveViewport Perspective
MaxViewport
ZoomExtents
SelLast
Crease
5,0,0
5,10,0
Enter
#narrate: A crease keeps an edge sharp. We select the box, run Crease, and pick the edges we want to keep crisp.
SelLast
What
#narrate: What describes the selection, which is now a SubD with a creased edge.
SelLast
ExtrudeSubD
5,5,10
Enter
3
@expect_objects 1
#narrate: ExtrudeSubD pulls a face outward. We pick the top face and extrude three units, and the smoothing reshapes the whole form around it.
ZoomExtents
SelLast
ToNURBS
@expect_objects 2
#narrate: When you need exact geometry, ToNURBS converts the SubD to a NURBS surface. The SubD stays, so you can keep editing the original.
