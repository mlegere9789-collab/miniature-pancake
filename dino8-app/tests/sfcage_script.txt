# ToSubD of a plain NURBS surface (the "SSC_" section, tests/sections/85_sfcage.sh).
#
# A Surface (Plane, SrfPt, an extruded arc...) used to be tessellated (an adaptive triangle mesh, two triangles for a flat plane) and handed to
# the SubD as its cage, with nothing interpolated and nothing measured. It now gets a quad cage on its own (u, v) grid, the cage is
# interpolated onto the surface and the limit surface is measured against the exact surface.
# Fixtures (the active CPlane is the world XY plane in the Top viewport):
#   plane      Plane 0,0,0 12,12,0                     a flat 12 x 12 sheet
#   twisted    SrfPt 0,0,0 10,0,0 10,10,10 0,10,0      the bilinear patch z = x y / 10
#   cylinder   Arc 0,0,0 10,0,0 0,10,0 then ExtrudeCrv 10: a quarter of a radius-10 cylinder, 10 high (a rational surface)
# smoke.sh runs this script; there is no @TMP@ substitution here.

SetActiveViewport Top

# ---- flat plane: the first cage (6 x 6 cells) already holds the limit surface exactly on the plane
New
SSCBEGIN_plane
Plane 0,0,0 12,12,0
SelLast
ToSubD
SelLast
SelSubDVertices
Enter
SelLast
SelSubDVertices
Type=Corner
Enter
SelLast
SelSubDVertices
Type=Crease
Enter
SelLast
SelSubDVertices
Type=Smooth
Enter
SelLast
SelSubDEdges
Type=All
Enter
SelLast
SelSubDEdges
Type=Boundary
Enter
SelLast
SubDVertexInfo
4,6,0
12,12,0
Enter

# ---- the bilinear patch z = x y / 10
New
SSCBEGIN_twisted
SrfPt 0,0,0 10,0,0 10,10,10 0,10,0
SelLast
ToSubD
SelLast
SubDVertexInfo
5,5,2.5
10,10,10
Enter
# one Catmull-Clark step: the new vertices lie BETWEEN the old cage vertices, so their limit points probe the surface off the interpolated points
SelLast
SubDivide
SelLast
SubDVertexInfo
2.1,3.3,0.7
6.4,7.9,5.1
8.8,1.4,1.3
Enter

# ---- a quarter cylinder: a curved, rational surface
New
SSCBEGIN_cylinder
Arc 0,0,0 10,0,0 0,10,0
SelLast
ExtrudeCrv
10
SelLast
ToSubD
# (List, not a sub-object selection: selecting all 2401 vertices of this cage makes every later viewport frame rebuild the control net per selected element)
SelLast
List
SelLast
SubDVertexInfo
10,0,5
7.0711,7.0711,5
Enter
