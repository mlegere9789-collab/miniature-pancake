# Cap option of Sweep1 / Sweep2 and the exact closed circular rail (tests/smoke.sh section SCP_).
# Every figure is MEASURED by the app and compared in smoke.sh with the analytic value. The smoke section cuts the output
# apart per case at each "Command: Sweep" line (cases, in order):
#  1 Sweep1 Cap=Yes, circle r=2 down a straight rail of 10 (cylinder, volume 40 pi = 125.664)
#  2 the same without Cap (stays an uncapped surface: default unchanged)
#  3 Sweep1 Cap=Yes, the circle sitting HALF WAY along the rail (rail split, both halves swept and joined, then capped)
#  4 Sweep2 Cap=Yes, circle r=2 between two straight rails converging 2 -> 1 (cone frustum, volume 70/3 pi = 73.304)
#  5 Sweep1 Cap=Yes with TWO circles r=2 / r=1 (Brep::Sweep1Sections wall, capped: the same frustum)
#  6 Sweep1 Cap=Yes with an OPEN section: refused with the reason, the uncapped surface is kept
#  7 Sweep1 Cap=No: circle section r=1 on a circle rail R=10 (torus; surface of revolution, ClosestPt of 5 exact torus points)
#  8 Sweep1 Cap=Yes on the same closed rail: closed torus solid, volume 2 pi^2 R r^2 = 197.392
#  9 control: an ELLIPSE rail (not a circle) keeps the kernel's ordinary skin
# 10 Sweep1 Cap=Yes with History On, then the section moved: the capped solid is rebuilt (still a solid)
