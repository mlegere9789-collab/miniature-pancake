# FilletSrf / ChamferSrf between two surfaces (Trim=No) record construction history (tests/smoke.sh section H3R_, part 4).
# With History On the fillet is recorded as {surface A, surface B} with the picked face indices and the radii; the two
# sources are not touched. Moving either surface rebuilds the fillet automatically (no UpdateHistory), EditHistory changes the
# radius, and a rebuild that cannot be done keeps the old fillet. Trim=Yes (which cuts the sources) is not recorded.
# Every claim is a measured Bounding box / Area, compared with the SAME fillet built fresh (History Off) between the
# surfaces in their moved positions.
# @TMP@ is the test temp directory.
# Bounding boxes, in order (Area in order of the Area lines):
#  1 fillet radius 1 between the floor and the wall   2 wall moved +3 X   3 fresh control (History Off) (= 2)
#  4 EditHistory radius=2   5 fresh control radius 2 (= 4)   6 reopened, wall moved +2 X more   7 fresh control (= 6)
#  8 a source is deleted: refused, old fillet kept (= 6)   9 ChamferSrf (ruled)   10 chamfer after the wall moved +2 X   11 fresh chamfer control (= 10)
#  (Trim=Yes cuts the sources: nothing is recorded, the History listing stays empty)
