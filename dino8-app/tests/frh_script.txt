# Exact fillet / chamfer of the rim of a cylindrical hole (see the "FRH" section at the end of smoke.sh).
#
# Every case starts from `New` (object ids restart at 1: the box is 1, the cutter 2, the Boolean result 3) and is
# bracketed by a marker line (an unknown command, "FRHBEGIN_<name>") that smoke.sh uses to slice this script's
# output into per-case blocks. The solid is a 6 x 6 x 4 box with a through-hole of radius 1.5 on its axis (cut by
# BooleanDifference, the exact General-engine result the app already produced): V0 = 144 - pi*1.5^2*4 = 115.726.
# A rim blend removes the closed-form (Pappus) volume
#   fillet radius r:            2*pi*r^2*(R + r/2 - (pi/4)*(R + r) + r/3)
#   chamfer wall w / cap c:     pi*w*c*(R + c/3)
