# History for the GENERAL (curved) and SEALED Boolean engine (see the "HGB_" section at the end of smoke.sh).
#
# With History On, BooleanUnion/BooleanDifference/BooleanIntersection of a box and a cylinder take the General
# engine (TryGeneralBrepBoolean + SealGeneralBooleanResult), hide the operands as the result's recorded sources and
# rebuild the sealed result when a source moves, scales or is changed (no UpdateHistory call anywhere). Every case
# starts from `New` (operands 1 and 2, result 3) and is bracketed by an unknown-command marker HGBBEGIN_<name> that
# smoke.sh slices the output on. The claims are measured numbers against closed forms:
