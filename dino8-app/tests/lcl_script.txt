# Loft Closed=Yes (tests/sections/63_lcl.sh, section LCL_): the kernel's closed Brep::Loft from the app.
# Eight unit circles (radius r = 1) in the eight radial planes of a ring of radius R = 5 (the plane through the world Z axis at
# 0, 45, ... 315 degrees, centre at distance 5 from the axis). Lofted on from the last circle back to the first they sample a
# torus; areas are read with the app's Area (a tessellated figure: the windows below allow the tessellation and the spline).
#   closed cubic    ~ torus area 4 pi^2 R r = 197.392 (the periodic cubic through the 8 sections is within about 1 percent of it)
#   open (control)  7 of the 8 gaps = 7/8 of that, 172.7
#   closed Straight (degree 1, exact ruled strips between neighbouring circles): 8 equal strips, each the integral over the unit
#                   circle of |dS/dtheta x dS/dt| of S = (1-t) P_i + t P_(i+1), computed outside the program by a midpoint rule
#                   (400 x 40 cells): 23.1386 per strip, 185.109 in all
#   History: the same closed loft edited to Style=Straight gives 185.109; edited on to Closed=No the open straight loft keeps 7 of
#                   the 8 strips, 161.97
# Every case starts from `New` and is bracketed by a marker line ("LCLBEGIN_<name>", an unknown command).
# Closed cases run with the DEFAULT options (SeamAlign=Yes): the circles are rotated copies of one circle, so the minimum-twist re-seam
# of a closed loft keeps every seam where it is; the control "noalign" runs the same ring with SeamAlign=No. The OPEN controls (the open
# loft, and the History edit to Closed=No) use SeamAlign=No: the open default still re-seams to the nearest point and twists a ring.
