# DraftAngleAnalysis median draft angle of a cone / cylinder wall (section 89_draftcone.sh, DCN_).
# A cone of base radius R and height H has a wall tilted atan(R/H) from its axis, so with the pull along the axis every wall normal
# makes draft = +-atan(R/H) (the sign says which way the apex points); a cylinder wall is parallel to the axis: draft 0.
# The wall carries most display vertices (the base cap is one flat face and the apex a single point), so the MEDIAN is the wall's draft.
# Order matters: the section reads the five reports back in this order.
SetActiveViewport Top
Cone 0,0,0 10 10
SelLast
DraftAngleAnalysis Direction=World
SelLast
DraftAngleAnalysis Direction=-Z
SelAll
Delete
Cone 0,0,0 5 10
SelLast
DraftAngleAnalysis Direction=World
SelAll
Delete
Cone 0,0,0 10 5
SelLast
DraftAngleAnalysis Direction=World
SelAll
Delete
Cylinder 0,0,0 5 20
SelLast
DraftAngleAnalysis Direction=World
SelAll
Delete
