# KWR: kernel capabilities wired into the app - NurbsSurface::Offset (exact analytic, tolerance-controlled
# otherwise) behind OffsetSrf of a surface, and the kernel cap-rim blends (ChamferCapRimAngle / FilletCapRim(s))
# behind ChamferEdge Distance/Angle and FilletEdge on a plain capped cylinder or cone frustum.
# Every case starts from `New` and is bracketed by an unknown-command marker ("KWRBEGIN_<name>") that smoke.sh
# uses to slice the output into per-case blocks. Closed forms (Pappus: V = 2*pi*rho*A for the (r,z) corner
# region cut away at a rim) are in smoke.sh next to each range.
# NB the app's Cone puts its APEX at the clicked base point and the flat cap at z = height; TCone 0,0,0 5 10 3 is
# the frustum r5 (z=0) -> r3 (z=10).
