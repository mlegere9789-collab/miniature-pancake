# Per-axis (non-uniform) scale of Sweep1: the ScaleX / ScaleY options (tests/smoke.sh section SPS_).
# "ScaleX=a,b" scales the section along its X axis by a at the rail start and b at the rail end, linear in the rail's arc
# length in between (ScaleY likewise along Y); the X axis is world X seen in the section plane at the rail start.
# Every figure is MEASURED by the app (ClosestPt on the finished surface, BoundingBox, the kernel's exact volume of the
# capped solid) and compared in smoke.sh with the closed form. The smoke section cuts the output apart per case at each
# "Command: Sweep1" line (cases, in order):
#  1 circle r=1 on a straight rail z = 0..10, ScaleX=1,2 ScaleY=1,0.5: elliptic frustum, semi-axes (1+f, 1-f/2) at z = 10 f;
#    ClosestPt of points ON the ellipses at z = 0, 5, 10 (distance ~0) and of the swapped-axes point (far)
#  2 the same with Cap=Yes: volume pi * 10 * integral (1+f)(1-f/2) df = 10 pi 13/12 = 34.0339
#  3 ScaleX=2,2 only (ScaleY untouched): elliptic cylinder, volume pi 2 1 10 = 62.8319
#  4 control, no scale option: the plain cylinder, volume 31.4159, and no per-axis message
#  5 THREE circles r=1 (z = 0, 5, 10) on the same rail with the scales of case 1, Cap=Yes: Brep::Sweep1Sections, ~34.034
#  6 closed circle rail R=10, circle r=1 at its start, ScaleX=1.5,1.5 ScaleY=0.8,0.8 Cap=Yes: volume 2 pi^2 R r^2 * 1.5 * 0.8
#  7 closed rail with a ramp ScaleX=1,2: refused by the kernel with its reason, no surface
#  8 section NOT at the rail start with a scale: refused with its reason, no surface
#  9 ScaleX=0,2: refused, no surface        10 ScaleX=-1,2: refused, no surface        11 ScaleY=abc: refused, no surface
