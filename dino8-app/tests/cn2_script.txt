# CN2_: G0 / G1 / G2 edge continuity against CURVED adjacent surfaces (see the "CN2_" section at the end of smoke.sh).
# cnt_script.txt only used a flat degree-1 plane. Here the adjacent surface is
#   * a 270 degree cylinder wall (radius 5, axis z, height 10; RATIONAL): `Revolve Angle=270` of the line (5,0,0)-(5,0,10);
#     its edge at 270 degrees is the vertical line x = 0, y = -5, picked with a click at 0,-5,5;
#   * a bicubic (non-rational, degree 3 x 1) wall made by the app's own Loft of four straight sections.
# Every case starts from `New` and is bracketed by an unknown-command marker "CN2BEGIN_<name>" that smoke.sh uses to slice the
# output. The "without the option" figure of each pair is the SAME construction with Continuity=Position (G0): the kernel
# glues the first section / curve to the edge but leaves the tangent plane alone, and reports the angle it MEASURED.
#
# Points on the cylinder (radius 5): 270 deg (0,-5)  285 (1.294095,-4.829629)  290 (1.710101,-4.698463)  300 (2.5,-4.330127)
#   310 (3.213938,-3.830222)  315 (3.535534,-3.535534)  330 (4.330127,-2.5)  345 (4.829629,-1.294095)
