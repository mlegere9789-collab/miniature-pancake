  move_wall_on    nudge one wall on a visible level

  cosmetic_on     edit a field nothing derives from (cutoff at the geometry)

  view_cut        change the cut height of one view
  type_edit       change the thickness of one wall type (about 1/types of all walls)

  level_on        change the elevation of a visible level (1/levels of all walls)
  tag_style       change the tag style (every tag)
  noop            set an input to its current value
