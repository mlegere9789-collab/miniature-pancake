# SRFSEC_: a single NURBS SURFACE object (not a B-rep) is cut by a clipping plane on the exact route too (it is wrapped into a
# one-face B-rep for kernel::SectionBrepByPlaneJoined), so the section is the true circle / arc, not a chord polygon of its render
# mesh. The surfaces are `ExtrudeCrv Solid=No` of a circle / a 270 degree arc of radius 5 (an exact rational degree 2 x 1 wall,
# listed as "(surface)"); cut at z = 4 the closed forms are the full circle 2 pi 5 = 31.4159265 and the OPEN arc 5 x 3 pi / 2 =
# 23.5619449. Every case starts from `New` and is bracketed by an unknown-command marker SRFBEGIN_<name> that
# sections/59_srfsec.sh slices the output on.
SetActiveViewport Top

Arc 0,0,0 5,0,0 0,-5,0
SelLast
Length digits=9
ExtrudeCrv Solid=No
10
SelID 1
Delete

ClippingSections
