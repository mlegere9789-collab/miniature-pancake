#title: Selection and snaps
#summary: Pick objects with the mouse, window and crossing selections, selection commands, and object snaps that lock clicks onto exact points.
#level: Beginner
#narrate: This tutorial is about two things: choosing objects, and clicking exactly where you mean. First, a small scene to work with.
SetActiveViewport Top
MaxViewport
Box 0,0,0 10,10,0 5
Box 20,0,0 30,10,0 5
Circle 15,25,0 4
Line 0,40,0 30,40,0
@expect_objects 4
ZoomExtents
#narrate: Two boxes, a circle and a line. Nothing is selected yet.
SelNone
@clickworld Top 10 5 0
@wait 3
@expect_selected 1
#shot
#narrate: Clicking an object in a viewport selects it. The click landed on the edge of the first box, and the Properties panel now describes the selection.
SelNone
SelWindow -1,-1,0 31,11,0
@expect_selected 2
#narrate: A window selection takes only the objects completely inside the rectangle. Both boxes qualify, the circle and line do not.
SelNone
SelCrossing -1,20,0 31,45,0
@expect_selected 2
#narrate: A crossing selection takes everything the rectangle touches. This one reaches the circle and the line.
SelNone
SelCrv
@expect_selected 2
#narrate: Commands can select by type. SelCrv picks every curve in the document, so the circle and the line.
Invert
@expect_selected 2
#narrate: Invert flips the selection, leaving the two boxes selected instead.
SelNone
@snap end 1
@snap mid 1
@snap cen 1
#narrate: Now snaps. End, Mid and Cen are on. Watch the Osnap bar: a click near an endpoint, midpoint or centre jumps to that exact point.
Line
@wait 2
@clickworld Top 0.4 40.3 0
@wait 2
@clickworld Top 15 25.5 0
@wait 2
@key Enter
@wait 2
@expect_objects 5
#narrate: We clicked slightly off the line's left end and slightly off the circle's centre. The new line starts and ends exactly on them.
SelLast
Length
#narrate: Length confirms it. A line from 0,40 to 15,25 is exactly twenty-one point two one units, with no rounding from where the mouse happened to be.
#narrate: Selection chooses what a command acts on, and snaps choose where. Together they cover most everyday modelling.
