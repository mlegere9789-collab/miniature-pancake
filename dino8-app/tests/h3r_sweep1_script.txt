# Sweep1 with SEVERAL cross-sections records construction history (tests/smoke.sh section H3R_, part 1).
# With History On the exact multi-section sweep is recorded as {rail, section 1, section 2}; editing the rail (here by
# moving two of its CONTROL POINTS, the way a gizmo drag does) or either section rebuilds the wall automatically (no
# UpdateHistory), in the same undo step. Every claim is a measured Bounding box / Area, compared with the SAME sweep built
# fresh (History Off) from the same, already moved, source curves.
# @TMP@ is the test temp directory.
# Bounding boxes, in order (Area in order of the Area lines):
#  1 fresh wall   2 section 2 moved +1 Y   3 section 1 moved -1 Y   4 section 1 moved -1 Y again, then Undo (= 3)
#  5 section 1 moved -1 Y again   6 rail control points 1 and 2 moved (HBarDragSelfTest)
#  7 fresh sweep from the same moved sources (History Off) (= 6)
#  8 reopened, section 1 moved +1 X   9 fresh control (= 8)   10 section 2 moved below the rail: refused, old wall kept (= 8)
#  11 History Off control: a section moved afterwards leaves the wall alone (= measurement 1)
