# Solids QC: Ellipsoid/SubDEllipsoid (real axis picking), Pyramid (NumSides=),
# Loft (Style=Straight), Cap (multiple separate openings) (see smoke.sh).
SetActiveViewport Top
# --- Ellipsoid: center, then the three semi-axis end points (5, 3, 2 along
# world x/y/z here); volume should be close to the analytic 4/3*pi*5*3*2.
Ellipsoid 0,0,0 5,0,0 0,3,0 0,0,2
SelLast
Volume
# --- SubDEllipsoid: same three axes, as a SubD/mesh approximation.
SubDEllipsoid 50,0,0 5,0,0 0,3,0 0,0,2
SelLast
List
# --- Pyramid: NumSides=6 gives a hexagonal-base mesh pyramid (4 is the default).
Pyramid 100,0,0 NumSides=6 5 10
SelLast
List
# --- Loft: three parallel lines. Normal fits a cubic-through-control-points
# v-direction (degree min(3, sections-1) = 2 here); Style=Straight drops to
# degree 1 (a real, linear/ruled surface, not just a smaller Normal fit).
SelAll
Hide
Line 150,0,0 160,0,0
Line 150,5,5 160,5,5
Line 150,0,10 160,0,10
SelCrv
Loft
SelSrf
List
SelAll
Hide
Line 150,0,0 160,0,0
Line 150,5,5 160,5,5
Line 150,0,10 160,0,10
SelCrv
Loft Style=Straight
SelSrf
List
# --- Cap: delete a box's top AND bottom faces (two separate, non-adjacent
# naked-edge loops), then Cap both back in with one call - previously Cap
# only ever capped a single loop per polysurface.
SelAll
Hide
Box 200,0,0 210,10,0 10
DeleteFaces
205,5,10
205,5,0
Enter
SelPolysrf
List
Cap
