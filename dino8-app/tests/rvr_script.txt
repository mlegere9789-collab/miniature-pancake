# Exact fillet / chamfer of ONE rim of a polygonal solid of revolution (see the "RVR_" section at the end of smoke.sh).
#
# kernel::FilletRevolveRim / ChamferRevolveRim are wired into FilletEdge / ChamferEdge (cmd_fillet.cpp) as the LAST exact
# attempt, after the capped-cylinder / frustum paths (FilletCapRim / ChamferCapRim) and the app's own cylinder / hole
# classifiers declined. The solid here is a capped cylinder (radius 5, height 4, axis z; V0 = 100 pi = 314.159) whose
# top rim was chamfered 1 x 1 by the app's own exact cone bevel: a 4-face solid of revolution (bottom cap, wall, cone

# (0,0) (5,0) (5,3) (4,4) (0,4), V1 = 299.498 (Green integral of the profile).
