# SELP_: SelSmall / SelID / SelChain argument parsing and reporting (sections/58_selparse.sh slices the output on the SELPBEGIN_ markers).
# Before: SelSmall silently replaced "abc", "-5", "1x" by the grid size; SelID dropped a non-numeric token without a word and counted a
# hidden or locked object as selected; SelChain on a selection with no curve ran anyway and reported the whole selection (box included)
# as "chained curve(s)". Everything below is hand-computed from the geometry written here.
SetActiveViewport Top

# SelSmall: 1-unit line (diagonal 1) and 100-unit line (diagonal 100); "SelSmall 2" selects exactly the first.
New

Line 0,0,0 1,0,0
Line 0,0,0 100,0,0
SelNone
SelSmall abc
SelNone
SelSmall -5
SelNone
SelSmall 1x
SelNone
SelSmall 2
@expect_selected 1
SelNone
SelSmall 200
@expect_selected 2
