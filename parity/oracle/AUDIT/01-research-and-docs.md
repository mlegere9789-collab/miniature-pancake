## 5. What is solid and should be kept

Row-by-row URLs and own-words statements (reviewers found 0-3 copy cases per sample); reproducible corpus baseline with all failures reported; ADR numbers faithful to spike reports; frank "what is not signed / not hosted / not concurrent" language in CODE_SIGNING, COMMUNITY, COMPUTE_SERVER; the ledger design (evidence-gated, computed, sealed by `spec_sha`); validator/percent tools with tests under `parity/tests`.
