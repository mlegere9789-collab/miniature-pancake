#title: Booleans on a bracket
#summary: Build a mounting bracket from a plate, a hole, a rib and a slot using boolean difference and union.
#level: Beginner
# Source: extends docs/tutorial_scripts/02_bracket.txt.
#narrate: Boolean operations add and subtract solids. We will build a small mounting bracket: a plate, a screw hole, a stiffening rib and a slot.
SetActiveViewport Top
Box 0,0,0 40,20,0 5
@expect_objects 1
#narrate: The plate is a box forty by twenty, five units thick.
Cylinder 10,10,-1 2 7
@expect_objects 2
#narrate: A cylinder of radius two, taller than the plate, will become the screw hole. It starts below and ends above the plate so the cut is clean.
SelID 1
BooleanDifference
#cont
SelID 2
Enter
@expect_objects 1
SelAll
Volume
#narrate: BooleanDifference subtracts the second object from the first. The cylinder is consumed, and Volume reports the plate minus the hole.
Box 0,0,4 40,4,4 16
@expect_objects 2
#narrate: A rib: a thin box standing along one long edge, overlapping the plate by one unit so the two solids truly share volume.
SelAll
BooleanUnion
@expect_objects 1
#narrate: BooleanUnion fuses everything selected into one solid. Plate and rib are now a single part.
SelAll
Volume
Box 25,8,-1 35,12,-1 8
SelNone
SelID 5
BooleanDifference
#cont
SelID 6
Enter
@expect_objects 1
#narrate: Finally a slot, cut with another box in the same way. Select the part, run BooleanDifference, then pick the cutter.
SelAll
Volume
#narrate: Three booleans, one finished bracket. The volume readouts in the command history show each step really removed or added material.
