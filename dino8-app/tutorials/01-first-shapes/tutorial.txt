#title: First shapes
#summary: Draw a box and a sphere, move and copy them, rotate, and undo - the basic loop of every Dino 8 session.
#level: Beginner
# Source: extends docs/tutorial_scripts/01_first_shapes.txt (same commands, plus narration).
#narrate: Welcome to Dino 8. This is the empty workspace: four viewports, the command line at the top, and the object snaps along the bottom.
SetActiveViewport Top
#narrate: Every action in Dino 8 is a command. We make the Top view active so the points we type land on the ground plane.
Box 0,0,0 10,10,0 5
@expect_objects 1
#narrate: The Box command takes two opposite corners and a height. Corners 0,0,0 and 10,10,0, five units tall. One object now exists in the document.
Sphere 20,0,5 5
@expect_objects 2
#narrate: A sphere takes a centre and a radius. It sits to the right of the box, and the document now holds two objects.
SelNone
SelID 1
#narrate: Objects are selected by clicking, or by a command. Here we select the box by its identifier, number one.
Move 0,0,0 5,5,0
#narrate: Move takes a start point and an end point. The box slides five units in X and five in Y.
SelLast
List
#narrate: List prints what is selected to the command history, so you can always check what a command really did.
SelNone
SelID 1
Copy 5,5,0 5,25,0
Enter
@expect_objects 3
#narrate: Copy works like Move but leaves the original behind. Pressing Enter ends the command, and we have three objects.
SelNone
SelID 3
Rotate 5,25,0 45
#narrate: Rotate takes a pivot point and an angle in degrees. The copy turns forty-five degrees about its own corner.
SelAll
List
Undo
Undo
@expect_objects 2
#narrate: Nothing is permanent. Two Undos step back through the rotate and the copy, and we are down to two objects again.
#narrate: That is the whole loop: pick a command, give it points, press Enter. The next tutorial covers selection and snaps.
