# Tutorial 2: Model a simple bracket - flat plate with a mounting hole and
# a rounded corner. Verifies the exact command sequence quoted in
# docs/site/tutorials.html "Model a simple bracket" tutorial.
SetActiveViewport Top
# 40x20x5 plate
Box 0,0,0 40,20,0 5
@expect_objects 1
# 4-unit-diameter mounting hole through the plate, centred at 10,10
Cylinder 10,10,-1 2 7
@expect_objects 2
SelID 1
BooleanDifference
SelID 2
Enter
@expect_objects 1
SelAll
Volume
# round one vertical corner edge of the plate
FilletEdge
Radius=3
40,20,2.5
Enter
@expect_objects 1
SelAll
Volume
