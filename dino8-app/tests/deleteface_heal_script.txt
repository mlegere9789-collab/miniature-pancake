# DeleteFaces Heal=Yes through the exact kernel heal (the "DFH_" section, tests/sections/84_deleteface_heal.sh).
#
# FacePickCommand's DeleteFaces gained the option Heal (default No: the old plain delete that leaves the solid open is unchanged). With
# Heal=Yes it calls kernel::DeleteFaceHealConvexPlanar: the picked face's plane is dropped and every neighbour regrows until they meet, so a
# chamfer disappears and the solid stays closed. When the kernel declines it says why (not planar, unbounded, not convex ...) and the face is
# simply deleted. Expected values are closed forms of a 10 x 10 x 10 cube (V = 1000, S = 600) whose top edges carry distance-2 chamfers:
#   one chamfer removes a 2 x 2 / 2 x 10 prism : 1000 - 20 = 980 ; healing it restores the cube : V = 1000, S = 600
#   two chamfers on OPPOSITE top edges (planes y + z = 18 and (10 - y) + z = 18 never overlap inside the cube) : 1000 - 40 = 960,
#     one heal gives 980, the second gives 1000
#   a plain cube's top face has nothing to regrow into : the kernel declines ("genuinely unbounded"), the face is deleted, 5 faces are left
#   a cylinder's wall is not planar : the kernel declines naming it
#   default (no Heal) : unchanged plain delete, 6 faces left, no kernel heal message
