# ChamferEdge / FilletEdge at a shared right-angle corner: two edges picked in one run that meet at a box corner (third edge sharp) are built together by the
# exact kernel corner functions (ChamferConvexEdgePairDistances, FilletConvexEdgePairMitre) instead of being chained one by one. Chained, the SMALLER chamfer
# picked first is refused (the second, larger chamfer's end would meet two faces) and equal fillets are refused outright (a corner of two fillets).
# Closed forms, 10x10x10 box, L_A = L_B = 10 (a = 1 - pi/4):
#   chamfer pair, d_A and d_B (legs equal per edge): V = 1000 - d_A^2/2 L_A - d_B^2/2 L_B + d_A d_B m - (d_A + d_B) m^2/2 + m^3/3, m = min(d_A, d_B)
#     d_A = 1 (vertical edge), d_B = 2 (top edge): 1000 - 5 - 20 + 2 - 1.5 + 1/3 = 975.8333
#   fillet pair, radius r: V = 1000 - a r^2 (L_A + L_B) + r^3 (5/3 - pi/2); r = 2: 1000 - 17.168 + 0.767 = 983.599 (the app's Volume command reads the display
#     tessellation of the two quarter-cylinders, about 0.1 lower: 983.5; without the overlap term it would be 982.83, one fillet alone 991.4; the exact
#     kernel volume is checked to the closed form in dino8-kernel/tests/extra/fillet_pair_mitre.cpp)
SetActiveViewport Top
# --- Smaller chamfer FIRST (distance 1 on the vertical edge, then 2 on the top edge): the second pick meets the first chamfer's face at the corner.
Box 1400000,0,0 1400010,10,0 10
ChamferEdge
Radius=1
1400010,10,5
Radius=2
1400004,10,10
Enter
SelAll
Volume
List
@expect_objects 1
SelAll
Delete
