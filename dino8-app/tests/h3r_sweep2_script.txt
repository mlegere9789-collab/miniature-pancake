# Sweep2 with SEVERAL cross-sections records construction history (tests/smoke.sh section H3R_, part 2).
# With History On the exact multi-section two-rail sweep is recorded as {rail 1, rail 2, section 1, section 2}; editing a
# section, or a rail by moving two of its CONTROL POINTS (the way a gizmo drag does), rebuilds the wall automatically (no
# UpdateHistory), in the same undo step. Every claim is a measured Bounding box / Area, compared with the SAME sweep built
# fresh (History Off) from the same, already moved, source curves.
# @TMP@ is the test temp directory.
# Bounding boxes, in order (Area in order of the Area lines):
#  1 fresh wall   2 section 2 moved +1 Y   3 section 1 moved -1 Y   4 section 1 moved -1 Y again, then Undo (= 3)
#  5 rail 2 control points 1 and 2 moved (HBarDragSelfTest)   6 fresh sweep from the same moved sources (History Off) (= 5)
#  7 reopened, section 2 moved +1 Y   8 fresh control (= 7)
#  9 a section moved onto the first rail's start (two sections at one station): refused, old wall kept (= 7)
#  10 History Off control: a section moved afterwards leaves the wall alone (= measurement 1)
