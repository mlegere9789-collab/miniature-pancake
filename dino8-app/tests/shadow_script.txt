# Real-time shadow maps QC (per-light shadow atlas): a flat floor slab, one
# box floating above its centre, and two DirectionalLights travelling in
# different horizontal directions as they shine down. GlRenderer now gives
# every enabled light its own real shadow-map layer (GlRenderer::
# BeginShadowPass(light_index, ...)), not just a single documented
# shadow-caster, so both lights' own shadows should land on the floor at
# once, in two different places. This script only builds the scene and
# captures a screenshot; smoke.sh does the actual pixel-level verification.
# @TMP@ is replaced by smoke.sh with a scratch directory.
Box -50,-50,-2 50,50,-2 2
SelLast
SetRenderColor 130,130,130
Box -5,-5,5 5,5,5 10
SelLast
SetRenderColor 40,140,40
# Light 1 travels down and toward -X as it enters the scene, so it casts
# the floating box's shadow onto the floor on the -X side. Intensity is
# deliberately low (two near-vertical lights at the default 1.0 would wash
# the whole frame out past this renderer's own tonemap knee).
DirectionalLight 0,0,30 -15,0,0 Intensity=0.4
# Light 2 travels down and toward -Y, casting the floating box's shadow
# onto the floor on the -Y side - a different place from Light 1's shadow.
DirectionalLight 0,0,30 0,-15,0 Intensity=0.4
GroundPlane Off
RenderedViewport
Top
ZoomExtentsAll
ViewCaptureToFile @TMP@/shadow_both.bmp
@expect_objects 2
